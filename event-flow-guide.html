Phase 0
Pre-Event Setup
Arrival & staging before doors open at 8:00 AM
Target: 6:30–7:45 AM
All staff must arrive no later than 7:00 AM. Matthew Dow (Event Director) leads setup walk-through at 7:15 AM.
1
Venue Walk-Through & Oche Setup Matthew Dow

Confirm oche tape, board heights, lighting rigs, and stage positions are correct. Verify CueLight system is online and boards 1 & 2 are calibrated.

2
Audio / Stream & Microphone Check Dana Moss Matthew Dow

Test PA / handheld mic levels in the venue. Confirm stream encoder is live and lower-third graphics overlay is loading correctly.

3
Scorekeeper Tablet Verification All Scorekeepers

Each scorekeeper (Aubrey Holland, Zach Davis, Tyler Stewart, Cecil Dow, Steve Rushton, Cory Wallace) logs into Score Keepers Portal and verifies the match schedule is loaded and accessible.

4
Door Station Setup Door Team

Kayla Melanson, Tanya Holland, Marie-Therese, and Dawn LeBlanc set up entry table: wristband stock, ticket scanner access, and cash float. Connie Dow opens Staff Portal for digital ticket verification.

5
Security Brief & Perimeter Check Jason Cole

Jason Cole confirms alcohol service boundary zones, emergency exit access, and reviews crowd control plan with Matthew Dow.

Go/No-Go Check at 7:45 AM: Matthew Dow confirms all stations are live. If any system is not ready, play schedule may be delayed — director call only.

Standings Calculation
Phase 4
Standings Tabulation
2:00 PM · 4th-Place Tiebreaker if required
1
Final Round Robin Results Lock Matthew Dow Scorekeepers

All scorekeepers submit final Round Robin match results simultaneously. Matthew Dow locks the scoring portal to freeze the standings snapshot.

2
Standings Verification (2-minute window) Matthew Dow Dana Moss

Confirm top 4 positions. Check for any tie at 4th place. Director and MC align on bracket seedings before any public announcement.

3
4th-Place Playoff (If Required) Caller

If two players are tied for 4th, a Best-of-3 tiebreaker match is played immediately — First to 2 legs. No walkout staging. Result determines the 4th qualifier.

Tiebreaker Format

Best of 3 legs · First to 2 · No walkout · Assigned caller only · Scorekeeper submits result immediately upon completion.

Phase 9
Awards, Trophy & Closing
Post-Final · Trophy presentation, interviews, farewell
Trophy Presentation Matthew Dow Dana Moss

Matthew Dow presents the AADS Champion of Champions trophy on the floor. Dana Moss announces the champion to the crowd. Stream continues live.

Champion Interview Denis Cormier

Denis Cormier conducts the official post-championship winner interview on camera. 3–5 minutes. Denis Leblanc provides broadcast wrap commentary simultaneously.

50/50 Draw Announcement Dana Moss Tanya Holland

Dana Moss draws and announces the 50/50 raffle winner on the PA. Tanya Holland coordinates the ticket reconciliation and payout.

Sponsor & Volunteer Acknowledgements Dana Moss Matthew Dow

Dana Moss thanks Amherst PharmaChoice (presenting sponsor), the Scoudouc Golden Age Club, all volunteers, and the 6 competing champions. Matthew Dow delivers any closing director remarks.

Stream Sign-Off Matthew Dow

Matthew Dow gives the signal to end the live stream. Denis Leblanc delivers the final broadcast sign-off. Stream closes on the trophy / champion shot.

Post-Event Teardown

All staff assist with venue restore. Scorekeeping tablets are collected and signed out. All event data (match results, ticket records) confirmed saved to cloud before departure. Matthew Dow signs off final operational completion.
