●Overview

The Score Keepers Portal is a simple self-serve schedule lookup for scorekeepers working a live event. It doesn't score matches itself — it just tells you when and where you're working, and pings your phone the moment your assigned match goes live or the schedule changes.

You sign in once with your name and a 4-digit code you pick yourself, turn on notifications, and from then on the portal just sits there quietly updating itself every few seconds — no refreshing, no re-logging-in.

The flow is the same every time you use it:

  1. Sign in (or create an account the first time)
  2. Turn on notifications so you don't have to keep checking
  3. Check My Shifts to see what you're working and its live status
  4. Use Full Schedule if you need to see everyone else's shifts too

1.Signing In / Creating an Account

This is the screen you land on the very first time you open the portal, or any time you've signed out.

If you already have an account, enter your name exactly as it appears on the schedule and your 4-digit code, then tap Sign In.

If this is your first time, tap Don't have an account? Create one and fill in your first name, last name, and a 4-digit code of your choosing — that code becomes your sign-in code from then on. Tap Create Account to finish.

Name must match the schedule. Your shifts are matched up by the exact name on your account, so use the same first/last name spelling the schedule organizer used (e.g. "Sarah Mitchell", not a nickname). If your shifts aren't showing up later, a name mismatch is the most likely reason.
No account found? If sign-in says it can't find an account with that name, you haven't created one on this device/browser yet — use Create one below instead. If you already created one elsewhere, just create it again here; it's the same account either way as long as the name matches.

You stay signed in between visits — there's no need to sign in again each time you reopen the page, unless you've tapped Sign Out.

2.Enable Notifications

Shown right after you sign in, if notifications aren't already turned on.

Tap 🔔 Enable Notifications and accept the browser's permission prompt. This is what lets the portal alert you the moment the schedule changes or your match goes live — without it, you'd have to keep the page open and watch it yourself.

Not ready yet? Tap Skip for now to move on without it — you can turn notifications on later from your status card (see next section).

On iPhone, notifications only work once the page has been added to the Home Screen as an app. If you try to enable notifications on iPhone before doing that, you'll get a reminder to tap 📲 Add to Home Screen first, then reopen the portal from that home screen icon and try again.

3.Your Status Card

The card at the top of the main screen, visible the whole time you're signed in.

Shows your name and an Alerts On / Alerts Off badge so you can tell at a glance whether notifications are working. If they're off, a 🔔 Enable Notifications button appears here so you can turn them on without signing out and back in.

If your browser supports installing the page as an app and hasn't already been added, a 📲 Add to Home Screen button also appears — worth doing, since it makes notifications more reliable (especially on iPhone) and gives you a one-tap icon for event day.

Tap Sign Out to log out of the portal on this device — you'll need your name and 4-digit code again to sign back in.

4.My Shifts

The default tab below your status card — this is the one you'll check most.

Lists only the shifts assigned to your name on the schedule, in the same order they were entered (not re-sorted), each showing its time, role, and the match it's tied to if there is one.

Every shift carries a live status pill on the right — see the cheat sheet below for what each one means. The card updates itself automatically every few seconds as the event progresses, so you never need to refresh.

Up next? A shift that's about to start gets highlighted with a soft orange background and an Up Next pill, so it's easy to spot which one needs your attention soon.
"No shifts found for you on the schedule yet." This means either the schedule hasn't been loaded by the event admin yet, or your account name doesn't exactly match the name used on the schedule. Double-check the spelling matches (see the warning in the Sign In section), and check back once the schedule is published.

5.Full Schedule

The second tab — tap Full Schedule in the tab bar to switch to it.

Shows every scorekeeper shift for the event, not just yours — useful for finding a swap, checking who else is working a session, or seeing the bigger picture. Same live status pills apply here too.

Rows for completed shifts are dimmed and struck through so the list stays readable as the event moves on.

★Tips & Troubleshooting

  • The portal polls automatically. Every few seconds it quietly checks for schedule or match-status changes and re-draws the screen if anything changed — you never need to pull-to-refresh or reload the page.
  • Notifications didn't fire? Check your status card for the Alerts On/Off badge. If it says Off, tap 🔔 Enable Notifications again — on iPhone, make sure the page was opened from the Home Screen icon, not the browser.
  • Forgot your 4-digit code? There's no reset flow in the portal — contact the event admin to confirm your name and code, or create a fresh account using a slightly different name if you truly can't recover it.
  • Shifts not showing up? It's almost always a name mismatch between your account and the schedule — check capitalization and spelling against what's printed on the official schedule.
  • Multiple devices? You can sign in with the same name and code on more than one device (e.g. phone and tablet) — both will poll and show the same live status independently.
  • Switching devices on event day? Notifications and "Add to Home Screen" are per-device — if you sign in somewhere new, you'll need to enable notifications there too.

📋Status Pill Cheat Sheet

PillWhat it means
Live NowThis shift's match is currently being played — you're on.
WaitingThis match is up but currently paused/holding (e.g. between legs or players not ready yet).
Up NextThis is the next match coming up — the row is also highlighted in orange so it stands out.
DoneThis shift's match has already finished.
ScheduledBooked on the schedule but not yet tied to a live match, or its match hasn't started moving through the order yet.
On BreakA break shift with no match attached — just a scheduled rest period.

Status updates automatically as the event runs — you don't need to do anything to refresh it.
