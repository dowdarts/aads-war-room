●Overview

The Interview Assistant tracks each match leg-by-leg as it happens, then automatically writes up to 4 custom interview questions based on what actually happened in that specific match — a comeback, a big finish, a string of 180s, how it compares to that player's earlier result, who's coming up next, and so on.

You don't need to do anything with stats or scoring math — just tap the player who did something notable and tap the matching action button. The app builds the story for you.

The flow for every match is the same four steps:

  1. Pick the match from the list
  2. Log events & leg winners as the match is played
  3. When the match ends, generate the interview questions
  4. Run the interview against the 3-minute timer, then mark it done

1.Picking a Match

This is the screen you land on when you open the app.

The list is split into Completed (matches already finished, shown with a ✓ and the final score) and Upcoming (not yet played). Tap any upcoming match to highlight it in orange, then tap Start Selected Match at the bottom.

Match formats

Each match's format is set automatically based on its position in the schedule — you don't need to choose it:

MatchesStageFormat
1–15Round RobinBest of 7 — first to 4 legs
164th Place TiebreakBest of 3 — first to 2 legs

No Event Configured? That message means the player list and match schedule haven't been loaded yet — that's set up separately by the admin (Matthew), not something you need to fix. Just check back once it's ready.

Resuming a match

If the app was closed or crashed mid-match, an orange Match in progress banner appears at the top with Discard and Resume buttons — tap Resume to pick back up exactly where you left off (score, legs, and events all restored).

2.Logging the Match Live

This is where you'll spend most of your time during a match.

At the top is the scoreboard: both player names, the current leg score (not points — legs won), and which leg you're on. The leader's number turns orange.

Step 1 — Pick the player

Before logging anything, tap the player name button for whoever the moment belongs to. The button highlights orange when selected. If you tap an action without picking a player first, both buttons flash orange as a reminder.

Step 2 — Tap the action

Tap whichever button matches what just happened (180, High Finish, Comeback, etc — full list in the cheat sheet below). Three of the actions open a tiny prompt asking for a detail:

  • Big Finish — type the checkout number (e.g. 146)
  • Low Dart Leg — type how many darts the leg took (e.g. 15)
  • Clutch Shot — type a short description (e.g. Double 16 to hold)

Everything you log appears in the event list right below, and can be removed individually with the ✕ if you tap the wrong thing.

Step 3 — Mark the leg winner

Once a leg finishes, tap that player's name under "Who won this leg?". The score updates, the leg gets filed into Leg History below, and the event log clears ready for the next leg.

That's it — repeat steps 1–3 for every notable moment, every leg, until the match reaches its target leg count. The app then automatically jumps you to the Winner screen — you don't tap anything to end the match.

If something's wrong

  • ↩ Undo Event — removes the last thing you logged in the current leg.
  • ↩ Undo Leg — un-does the most recently completed leg (use this if you tapped the wrong winner) and restores its events so you can re-log the ending correctly.
  • 💾 Save — the app already auto-saves every time you log something, but tap this any time you want to be sure.

Tap ← List in the header at any time to back out to the match list — your progress is saved as a draft automatically, so you can come back to it later from the Resume banner.

3.Winner Screen

Shown automatically the moment a match is decided.

Three options here:

  • Generate Interview Questions — the normal next step. Takes you to the questions + timer screen.
  • Edit Result — jumps back into the live logger for this match if the final score or a leg winner was wrong.
  • Back to Match List (no interview) — skips the interview for this match entirely (e.g. no time, or the player isn't available) and returns you to the match list.

4.Interview Questions & Timer

Your on-air reference for the interview itself.

The questions

Up to 4 questions appear automatically, picked based on what actually happened: how the match opened (a last-leg decider, a comeback, a dominant win, or a standard close one), plus whichever of these are relevant — a big/high finish, a clutch shot, multiple 180s, a low-dart leg, a winning streak, the opponent's missed doubles, an updated round-robin record, or a tease of who's up next. If none of those fit, two general fallback questions are always available.

Tap the ↻ button next to any question to cycle through 2–3 alternate phrasings of that same question — handy if the wording doesn't feel natural to say on air, or you've already half-covered it talking to the player off-camera.

The timer

A 3-minute countdown starts automatically as soon as you reach this screen. It turns red under 30 seconds, and at 0:00 the whole screen flashes red with a WRAP UP INTERVIEW banner — your cue to close it out. The timer is a guide, not a hard cutoff — it keeps running (and flashing) past zero, so there's no rush to cut someone off mid-sentence.

Ending it

Tap Done — End Interview when you're finished. This stops the timer and returns you to the match list, ready for the next match.

5.Standings

Tap Standings in the top-right of the match list screen at any time to see the current Round Robin table — position, name, province, win-loss record, and points (2 per win). Semifinal, tiebreak, and final matches don't affect this table. Tap outside the sheet (or Close) to dismiss it.

★Tips & Troubleshooting

  • The save dot next to "← List" in the logger header shows save status: grey = idle, orange = saving, green = saved. You never need to manually save, but it's there to reassure you it's working.
  • Lost connection or app closed mid-match? Nothing is lost — reopen the app, and the Resume banner on the match list will offer to pick the match back up exactly where it left off.
  • Wrong leg winner tapped? Use ↩ Undo Leg right away, before logging anything new — it un-does the leg and gives you back its events to fix.
  • Need to redo a finished match? Go to its Winner screen result and tap Edit Result — or if questions were already generated, you can still go back via the match list and select it again once it's marked incomplete.
  • Multiple devices? Drafts are saved to the cloud, not just your device — if you hand the tablet to someone else partway through a match, they'll see the same Resume banner.
